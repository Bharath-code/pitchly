# Pitchly — 3-Minute Loom Demo Script

**Audience:** Warm leads (SDR Managers, Sales Enablement Managers who replied to outreach)
**Tone:** Professional, confident, slightly conversational. Like you're showing a colleague something cool.
**Length:** 3 minutes (hard stop)

---

## Before You Record

### Setup Checklist

- [ ] Pitchly extension loaded in Chrome (`chrome://extensions` → Load unpacked → `extension/dist/`)
- [ ] Worker deployed and settings configured (Worker URL set to your .workers.dev domain)
- [ ] A friend or second computer ready to play "Prospect" on a Google Meet call
- [ ] Close all tabs except: Google Meet call + Pitchly popup
- [ ] Loom recording: **Camera + Screen + Mic** (camera adds trust)
- [ ] Have the script open on a second monitor or phone
- [ ] Do 1 practice run to verify timing

### Audio Note

Use a headset with a good mic. The person watching needs to hear:
1. Your voice clearly
2. The prospect's voice clearly
3. No background noise

---

## 🎬 The Script (0:00 – 3:00)

### 0:00 – 0:20 | The Hook

*Camera on you. Smile. Natural lighting.*

**You:** "Hey [Name], thanks for letting me send this over. I wanted to show you Pitchly in 3 minutes — because that's really all it takes to see if this is useful for your team."

*Brief pause. Lean slightly forward.*

**You:** "The problem I'm solving is simple: SDRs freeze when prospects raise objections. They know what to say, but in the moment, their brain goes blank. Pitchly gives them the script in real time — during the call, not after."

---

### 0:20 – 0:35 | Setup (Screen Share Starts)

*Switch to screen share. Show Chrome with the Google Meet tab and Pitchly popup pinned.*

**You:** "Here's what it looks like. I've got a Google Meet call open. I click the Pitchly icon, click Start Listening — one button — and the HUD appears."

*Hover mouse over the Pitchly popup. Click Start Listening. Show the status badge change to "Listening" and audio mode show "Tab + Mic".*

**You:** "That's it. Two clicks. The HUD slides into the bottom-right corner. You can see the talk ratio bar, sentiment dot, and it's listening for objections."

---

### 0:35 – 1:30 | The Magic Moment (Objection Detection)

*Switch to the Google Meet tab. Your friend (playing the prospect) should be visible in the call.*

**You:** "Let me show you what happens when the prospect raises an objection."

*Nod to your friend to start.*

**Prospect:** "Honestly, I don't think we need another tool right now. We're doing fine with what we have."

*Wait 2-3 seconds for the card to appear.*

**You:** *Point cursor at the card as it slides in.* "There it is. Two seconds after they finished speaking, Pitchly detected a 'no_need' objection. You can see the badge, the confidence score, and the response is typing in."

*Let the typing effect finish. Point at the blinking cursor.*

**You:** "That's real — it's streaming token by token from Gemini. The rep reads it, says it in their own words, and keeps the conversation moving."

*Point at the feedback buttons.*

**You:** "After the card locks in, the rep can tell us if it was helpful or not. That feedback makes the system smarter over time."

---

### 1:30 – 2:00 | A Second Objection (Show It's Not a Fluke)

**You:** "Let's try one more."

*Nod to your friend.*

**Prospect:** "That's all great, but how do I know your data is real? I've seen demo magic before."

*Wait for the card. Point at the "trust" badge.*

**You:** "Trust objection. Different type, different color, different response. Same 2-second reaction time."

*Pause for the text to stream in.*

**You:** "The response is pulled from a knowledge base of proven scripts — not hallucinated on the spot. Every card has a confidence score. If it's below 75%, we don't show it. No false alarms."

---

### 2:00 – 2:30 | The Post-Call Snapshot

**You:** "Now here's the part managers really love. When the call ends — "

*Click Stop Listening in the Pitchly popup.*

**You:** "— the post-call snapshot appears instantly."

*Point at the snapshot panel showing stats grid, objections, summary, follow-up draft.*

**You:** "Duration, talk ratio, sentiment over time. Every objection that was handled, with type and confidence. A full call summary generated by AI. And a follow-up draft they can copy with one click."

*Click the Copy button on the follow-up draft.*

**You:** "One click and it's on their clipboard. They paste it into Gmail and send it. No more 'I'll remember to follow up' and then forgetting."

*Show the persistence status.*

**You:** "And it's saved to our database — so managers can see objection patterns across their entire team over time."

---

### 2:30 – 3:00 | The Ask

*Switch back to camera (or stay on screen share with the dashboard visible).*

**You:** "So here's what I'm asking."

*Slow down slightly. Be direct.*

**You:** "Try it on 3 real calls this week. I'll set you up in 2 minutes — it's a Chrome extension, nothing to install on servers, no IT needed."

*Point cursor at the Pitchly icon.*

**You:** "If it doesn't help your reps handle objections better, you owe me nothing. If it does, we can talk about what a paid plan looks like."

*Smile. Wrap up.*

**You:** "Sound fair? I'll send you the install link. Let me know what you think."

*End recording.*

## 🎯 What to Optimize

| Element | Why It Matters | How to Nail It |
|---------|---------------|----------------|
| **First 10 seconds** | Viewer decides if they'll watch | Lead with the problem, not the product |
| **The objection moment** | This is the "holy sh*t" proof | Speak naturally, let the card surprise them |
| **Response streaming** | Shows it's real-time, not staged | Let the typing effect play out — don't talk over it |
| **The follow-up draft** | Managers care about this more than cards | Click the Copy button — show how easy it is |
| **The ask** | This is why you made the video | Be direct, be low-risk, make it easy to say yes |

## ⚡ Bloopers & Recovery Scripts

| If This Happens | Say This |
|----------------|----------|
| Card doesn't appear | *Keep going.* "Sometimes it takes 3 seconds. Let's try one more time — say that objection again." |
| Prospect laughs/stumbles | *Keep rolling.* "And as you can see, it works better when the prospect cooperates." *Laugh.* |
| Extension crashes | *Don't panic.* "Well, that's why it's a beta." *Reload the extension.* |
| You blank on the script | *Pause, look at your notes.* "Hold on, let me check my script. I want to get this right for you." — This is authentic. |
| Audio cuts out | *Stop. Restart Loom.* "Let me restart real quick." — Edit it out. |
