# Pitchly — Loom Demo Dry Run Guide

**Audience:** Warm leads (SDR Managers, Sales Enablement Managers)
**Length:** 3 minutes
**Setup:** Google Meet call with a friend playing "Prospect"

---

## Step 1: Set Up

Before hitting record:

- [ ] Pitchly extension loaded in Chrome (`chrome://extensions` → Load unpacked → `extension/dist/`)
- [ ] Worker URL set to deployed worker in the popup settings
- [ ] Friend/colleague on a Google Meet call (acting as prospect)
- [ ] Loom set to **Cam + Screen + Mic** recording mode
- [ ] Script visible on phone or second monitor

---

## Step 2: Record — Section by Section

### 🐚 HOOK (0:00)

*Camera on you. Smile.*

> "Hey [Name], thanks for letting me send this over. I wanted to show you Pitchly in 3 minutes — because that's really all it takes to see if this is useful for your team."
>
> *Brief pause.*
>
> "The problem I'm solving is simple: SDRs freeze when prospects raise objections. They know what to say, but in the moment, their brain goes blank. Pitchly gives them the script in real time — during the call, not after."

---

### ⚙️ SETUP (0:20)

*Switch to screen share. Show Chrome with Google Meet tab + Pitchly popup.*

> "Here's what it looks like. I've got a Google Meet call open. I click the Pitchly icon, click Start Listening — one button — and the HUD appears."

*Action: Click Pitchly icon → Click "Start Listening" → Wait for "Listening" badge and "Tab + Mic" indicator.*

> "That's it. Two clicks. The HUD slides into the bottom-right corner. You can see the talk ratio bar, sentiment dot, and it's listening for objections."

---

### ✨ FIRST OBJECTION (0:35)

*Switch to Google Meet tab. Nod at friend to start.*

> **Friend:** "Honestly, I don't think we need another tool right now. We're doing fine with what we have."

*Wait 2-3 seconds for the card.*

> "There it is. Two seconds after they finished speaking, Pitchly detected a 'no_need' objection. You can see the badge, the confidence score, and the response is typing in."
>
> "That's real — it's streaming token by token from Gemini. The rep reads it, says it in their own words, and keeps the conversation moving."
>
> "After the card locks in, the rep can tell us if it was helpful or not. That feedback makes the system smarter over time."

---

### 🔁 SECOND OBJECTION (1:30)

> "Let's try one more."

*Nod at friend.*

> **Friend:** "That's all great, but how do I know your data is real? I've seen demo magic before."

*Wait for card. Point at the "trust" badge.*

> "Trust objection. Different type, different color, different response. Same 2-second reaction time."
>
> "The response is pulled from a knowledge base of proven scripts — not hallucinated on the spot. Every card has a confidence score. If it's below 75%, we don't show it. No false alarms."

---

### 📊 POST-CALL SNAPSHOT (2:00)

> "Now here's the part managers really love. When the call ends —"

*Click "Stop Listening" in popup.*

> "— the post-call snapshot appears instantly."

*Point at elements as you name them:*

> "Duration, talk ratio, sentiment over time. Every objection that was handled, with type and confidence. A full call summary generated by AI. And a follow-up draft they can copy with one click."

*Click the Copy button on the follow-up draft.*

> "One click and it's on their clipboard. They paste it into Gmail and send it. No more 'I'll remember to follow up' and then forgetting."
>
> "And it's saved to our database — so managers can see objection patterns across their entire team over time."

---

### 🎯 THE ASK (2:30)

*Switch back to camera.*

> "So here's what I'm asking."

*Slow down slightly.*

> "Try it on 3 real calls this week. I'll set you up in 2 minutes — it's a Chrome extension, nothing to install on servers, no IT needed."
>
> "If it doesn't help your reps handle objections better, you owe me nothing. If it does, we can talk about what a paid plan looks like."
>
> *Smile.*
>
> "Sound fair? I'll send you the install link. Let me know what you think."

*End recording.*

## ⚡ Bloopers & Recovery

| If This Happens | Say This |
|----------------|----------|
| Card doesn't appear | "Sometimes it takes 3 seconds. Let's try one more time." |
| Friend stumbles | "And as you can see, it works better when the prospect cooperates." (laugh) |
| Extension crashes | "Well, that's why it's a beta." (reload) |
| You blank on lines | Pause, look at notes. "Let me get this right for you." — authentic. |
| Audio cuts out | Stop. Restart Loom. Edit it out. |

---

*Record twice. First take loosens you up. Second take is the keeper.* 🚀
