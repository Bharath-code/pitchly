## 🎯 Key Takeaways

1. **Personalize every message** — Reference something specific about their company or recent post
2. **Keep DMs under 150 characters** — People read DMs on mobile. Short = read. Long = ignored.
3. **Offer value, don't pitch** — The first message should be a question, not a feature list
4. **Follow up 3x max** — If they don't reply after 3 messages, move on
5. **Record the Loom first** — Your single most important marketing asset is a 3-minute demo showing Pitchly working on a real call
6. **Talk to 50 people to get 10 users** — The conversion math is ~20% from DM to call, ~50% from call to user
